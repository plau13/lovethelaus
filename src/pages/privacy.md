This Privacy Policy describes how **PAL CAPITAL, LLC** ("**Kitchen**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Kitchen website, web app, and any related mobile applications (collectively, the "**Service**").

By using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. See also our [Terms of Service](/terms).

## Who we are

**Operator:** PAL CAPITAL, LLC  
**Contact:** privacy@lovethelaus.com  
**Website:** https://lovethelaus.com/kitchen

## Information we collect

### Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

- Email address
- Display name
- Password (stored by our authentication provider; we do not store plain-text passwords)
- Kitchen preferences (default servings, units)

### Content you provide

- Recipes (ingredients, steps, tags, photos)
- Cookbooks and sharing settings
- Notes and collaboration activity
- Recipe version history when edits are saved

### Usage and import data

- Social import counts (Instagram/TikTok link imports)
- Subscription tier and feature usage
- Basic server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps)

### Device and offline data (subscribers)

If you have a subscription with offline cook mode, recipe data you choose to cache may be stored locally on your device. We do not receive that cached data unless you sync while online.

## How we use information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain the Service
- Authenticate you and manage your account
- Process recipe imports (including caption text and, when available, video analysis)
- Enforce free and paid feature limits
- Send magic-link and password-reset emails
- Respond to support requests
- Improve reliability and security

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Cookies, analytics, and advertising

**Signed-in app.** Kitchen sets a session cookie so you stay signed in. It contains no tracking identifiers and is not shared with advertisers. There are no ads and no third-party analytics inside the app, in cook mode, or on any page that requires an account.

**Public pages.** Recipes and cookbooks that a family has chosen to make public (for example `/kitchen/explore` and public cookbook links) may show advertising served by Google AdSense and may be measured with Google Analytics. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites, and to measure how ads perform. Where required by law you will see a consent choice before any advertising cookies are set, and you can change it at any time from the consent link on those pages.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com), and learn more or opt out of other vendors at [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) and [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/). Google's use of data is described at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Sharing and collaboration

When you share a cookbook or grant recipe access (view, comment, or edit), other Kitchen users you invite can see the content you share according to the permissions you set. You control sharing in the app.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

## Data retention

We retain your account and recipe data while your account is active. When you delete your account in Settings, we delete your recipes, cookbooks, and notes from our systems, subject to backup retention for a limited period.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access, correct, or delete your personal data
- Export your recipes (see in-app export)
- Object to or restrict certain processing

Contact **privacy@lovethelaus.com** to exercise these rights.
