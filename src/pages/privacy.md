## Information we collect

### Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

- Email address
- Display name
- Password (stored by our authentication provider; we do not store plain-text passwords)
- Kitchen preferences (default servings, units)

### Content you provide

- Recipes (ingredients, steps, tags, photos)
- Cookbooks and sharing settings
- Notes and collaboration activity
- Recipe version history when edits are saved

### Usage and import data

- Social import counts (Instagram/TikTok link imports)
- Subscription tier and feature usage
- Basic server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps)

### Device and offline data (subscribers)

If you have a subscription with offline cook mode, recipe data you choose to cache may be stored locally on your device. We do not receive that cached data unless you sync while online.

## How we use information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain the Service
- Authenticate you and manage your account
- Process recipe imports (including caption text and, when available, video analysis)
- Enforce free and paid feature limits
- Send magic-link and password-reset emails
- Respond to support requests
- Improve reliability and security

We do **not** sell your personal information.

When you share a cookbook or grant recipe access (view, comment, or edit), other Kitchen users you invite can see the content you share according to the permissions you set. You control sharing in the app.
