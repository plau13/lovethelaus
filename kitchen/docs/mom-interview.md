# Mom interview (Phase 0)

Do this in person with the phone or iPad she cooks from. Capture answers in the app at `/interview` after you sign in.

## Script

1. What was the old recipe website called?
2. Where do recipes live now (screenshots, Notes, texts, a binder, email)?
3. Name 10 recipes you want in first. Cook from those this week.
4. What did the old site do that you still miss?
5. Show me how you find a recipe when dinner is in 20 minutes.
6. Is kitchen Wi-Fi reliable, or do we need recipes to work offline?
7. Do you want to print, cook from the iPad on the counter, or both?
8. Who else should add recipes (aunts, siblings)? Should they be able to change yours, or only leave a note?
9. Are you okay with a website on the Home Screen, or does it need an App Store icon?
10. Any photos of kids that must stay private if a cookbook is ever shared?

## Success

You leave with: old site name, current hiding places, 10 recipes, and whether print/offline/sharing matter in week one.
